## Decisions/Rationales

**Finding**: The canonical golden generator captures full calibration metadata (flux, beamsize, N_cells) into `config_torch.json`, but the runtime `simulate_forward_once` helper still instantiates nanobrag_torch Beam and Crystal configs from minimal input parameters.

**Implication**: Even with correct geometry, refined structure factors, and spot_scale_override applied, simulator physics diverge from calibrated capture because beam/crystal configuration mismatches cause different diffraction patterns and ROI peak localization.

**Next Step Recommendation**: Enhance bridge builders (`create_beam_config`, `create_crystal_config`) to load canonical calibration fields from config_torch.json before instantiating simulator components.
