## Decisions/Rationales

- **Root Cause:** LBFGS starts at scale=1.0 and takes an unconstrained step without line search. The masked loss magnitude (~1e6) combined with the unbounded `exp` parameterization allows catastrophic overshooting.
- **Solution Strategy:** Two-part fix:
  1. **Warm-start:** Initialize `log_scale` from the calibration hint (~62.66) per `docs/spec-db-workflow.md:20-40` guidance
  2. **Bounds:** Clamp the log-space parameter or constrain updates to keep scale finite during optimization
- **Evidence Priority:** The telemetry showing best snapshot at `log_scale≈4.29` (scale≈72) near the hint proves the simulator is otherwise healthy; the issue is purely parameterization/initialization.
