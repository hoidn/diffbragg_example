## Summary

Investigated Stage A refinement behavior with perturbed geometry using nearest-neighbor HKL sampling to measure improvement headroom and gradient flow. Discovered that while HKL telemetry remains healthy with ~98% in-bounds hit rate, LBFGS improvement stalls at 0.22% and orientation deltas remain zero because the baseline misset is dropped when crystal_overrides are used. Follow-on analysis with manually injected misset confirmed orientation gradient remains effectively zero even with nearest-neighbor sampling, reaching only 0.2-0.3% improvement.

## Decisions/Rationales

1. **Nearest-Neighbor Validation:** Confirmed fractional HKL access works fine once interpolation is disabled; 0% hit rate from prior attempts was due to integer index lookup rejection, not grid bounds violations

2. **Misset Preservation Issue:** Identified that `create_crystal_config` drops perturbed misset whenever `crystal_overrides` are supplied, causing orientation_vec to remain zero

3. **Gradient Source Analysis:** Even with manual misset injection, nearest-neighbor |F| lookup yields <0.3% improvement, implying orientation gradient is effectively zero without interpolation

4. **Implementation Strategy:** Remaining blocker is plumbing baseline misset into `misset_deg_override` and disabling interpolation in Stage A path, then reassessing whether additional perturbation amplitude or alternative gradient sources are required to reach ≥5% gate

## Errors/Failures

**No explicit errors reported** in iteration 90. Analysis revealed limitations rather than failures:

### Root Cause Analysis

1. **Zero Orientation Gradients with Nearest-Neighbor:**
   - Suspected cause: Without tricubic interpolation, structure factor lookups use nearest integer HKL, eliminating smooth gradients needed for orientation refinement
   - Evidence: Improvement stalled at 0.22-0.29% even with larger perturbations (+4° rotation)

2. **Misset Dropout in create_crystal_config:**
   - Suspected cause: When `crystal_overrides` dict is provided, baseline crystal's misset is not preserved/combined with override values
   - Evidence: `orientation_vec` delta = 0.0 in telemetry, `misset_xyz_deg` = [0.0, 0.0, 0.0]

3. **HKL Hit Rate Healthy but Improvement Poor:**
   - Observation: 97.84% in-bounds rate (h=[-17,23], k=[-22,22], l=[0,14]) confirms grid compatibility
   - Interpretation: Grid bounds are not the issue; lack of interpolation removes gradient information
