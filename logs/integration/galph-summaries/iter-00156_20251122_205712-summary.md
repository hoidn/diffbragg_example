**Parameterization Choice**: Selected quaternion (4-DOF unit norm) over axis-angle or Lie algebra approaches due to:
- Numerical stability (unit quaternion constraints are smooth)
- Gradient quality (quaternion-to-matrix derivatives are well-conditioned)
- Established PyTorch/scipy libraries for conversion and SO(3) operations
