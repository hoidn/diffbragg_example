## Decisions/Rationales

- **Quaternion approach ruled out**: Despite accepting ~4e-05 parity gap (same as cell+misset baseline), the catastrophic convergence failure (chi² +1257%) during Phase C2 optimization definitively demonstrates that SO(3) quaternion constraint is incompatible with the measurement geometry embedded in MOSFLM A*.
- **Root cause hypothesis**: det(U₀)=1.000557 indicates mapping A* contains ~0.06% isotropic volume scaling that SO(3) projection discards, creating pathological gradient flow. Three hypotheses identified: (H1) dxtbx A*/cell inconsistency, (H2) physical volume scaling in crystal (thermal/radiation effects), (H3) numerical precision in B_ideal derivation.
- **Hybrid parameterization path**: Rather than accept degraded convergence with pure quaternion, escalation chose to investigate root cause and design hybrid parameterization (e.g., s·R(q)@cell where s=det(U)^(1/3)) that preserves volume scaling while constraining rotation to SO(3).
- **Escalation rationale**: At maximum retry threshold (Phase C failure after Phase B regression bugfix), protocol requires escalation to new initiative with extended scope rather than continued incremental refinement.
