## Decisions/Rationales

**Key Finding:** The probe discovered 100% in-bounds coverage (69614/69614 reflections within baseline grid bounds h=[-24,24], k=[-28,28], l=[-31,31]), directly contradicting the REFINE-005 hypothesis that attributed the 0% hit rate to grid bounds violations.

**Anomaly Identified:** All 69614 perturbed indices are non-integer (max fractional part: 0.5, max deviation: 1.03 on h-axis), confirming that the basis transformation introduces fractional Miller indices.

**Revised Understanding:** The prior 0% hit-rate failure likely stemmed from the simulator's integer index lookup rejecting fractional coordinates, not from grid bounds violations. This shifts the investigation toward nanobrag_torch HKL interpolation semantics.
