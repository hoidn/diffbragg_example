## Decisions/Rationales

- Small detector is the default for smoke/perf loops to conserve GPU VRAM; parity/DB-AT selectors must explicitly set `--smoke-detector-size=full` or `DBEX_SMOKE_DETECTOR_SIZE=full` to ensure error budgets remain stable
- Cropped dataset lives under `sp.proc/refGeom_small/` to separate smoke assets from canonical refGeom, with README/checksums for provenance
- Test gates are gated on detector size: relaxed when `smoke_detector_size=="small"` (no loss-improvement assertion) to accommodate the smaller ROI count, strict when `=="full"` to maintain acceptance criteria parity
- Telemetry is written conditionally to `DBEX_SMOKE_TELEMETRY_PATH` (environment variable) for perf tracking without cluttering normal test runs
