# Decisions/Rationales

- **ROI-based caching strategy**: Eliminated per-panel Simulator rebuilds inside LBFGS closure by precomputing cropped DetectorConfigs per `inputs.panel_slices`, matching the specification requirement that warm mode reuses cached structures without redundant instantiation
- **Unified benchmark control**: Both warm and cold paths now employ ROI-only rendering to ensure comparable profiling; cold mode rebuilds ROI detectors rather than full-panel detectors to maintain diagnostics parity
- **Telemetry tracking**: Added ROI-aware counters to distinguish sampled regions from total available regions, enabling future analysis of ROI sampling efficiency and closure iteration statistics
