## Errors/Failures

### Test Failures (Expected / Diagnostic)

Both tests failed on their assertions due to measurement/calibration mismatch, NOT execution errors:

**DB-AT-028: test_db_at_028_loss_scale_sanity**
- **Failure**: `AssertionError: chi²/pixel initial 1.084e+05 exceeds 1e2 bound`
- **Measured value**: 108,367.84 chi²/pixel (initial)
- **Expected bound**: ≤ 100.0
- **Root cause (suspected)**: Mapping helper using golden fixture spot_scale_override (3.18e+17) instead of smoke dataset calibration; extreme scaling inflates chi² per pixel when applied to unmapped reflections.
- **Evidence**: `db_at_028_metrics.json` shows `spot_scale_override: 3.184711299019782e+17`, indicating golden calibration was loaded.

**DB-AT-029: test_db_at_029_structure_parity**
- **Failure**: `AssertionError: median ROI correlation before refinement -0.054 below 0.2 floor`
- **Measured value**: -0.054 median ROI correlation
- **Expected bound**: ≥ 0.2
- **Root cause (suspected)**: Negative correlations imply mapping geometry misalignment or structural mismatch between smoke dataset reflections and the golden fixture's coordinate frame. The fixture may use a different detector calibration or HKL reference.
- **Evidence**: `roi_metrics.json` reports 92 ROIs with median correlation -0.0399 (even lower than per-test measurements), some ROIs as low as -0.204.
