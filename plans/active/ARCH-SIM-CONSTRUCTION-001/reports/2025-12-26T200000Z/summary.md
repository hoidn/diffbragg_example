## Diagnosis
The SQUARE branch of `src/nanobrag-torch/src/nanobrag_torch/simulator.py::compute_physics_for_position` currently feeds `sincg(torch.pi * h, N)` with the raw Miller coordinates and float32 precision. Because `sincg` only special-cases arguments that are exactly within 1e-10 of an integer multiple of π, the float32 rounding errors at `h ≈ n ± 1e-4` fall outside that window and the ratio `sin(Nπh)/sin(πh)` collapses toward zero instead of ±N. The partiality ledger (and hook) therefore see near-zero `F_latt` everywhere even though the calibration advertises 41×29×32 mosaic domains, which explains the 10⁻³–10⁻⁵ Stage A/|F|²·F_latt²·LP ratios.
